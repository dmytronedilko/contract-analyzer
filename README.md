Contract Analyzer lets legal and finance teams upload multi-page PDF contracts, ask questions
about them and compare two contracts, with every statement cited to the page it comes from.
Answers are generated by Claude from excerpts retrieved with legal-domain embeddings; teams sign
in with single sign-on and work in organizations with role-based access. It is not legal
advice: always verify against the original document.

## Quick start

Requires Node 24, pnpm 11 and Docker. Put your Anthropic and Voyage AI keys in `apps/api/.env`
and a random `BETTER_AUTH_SECRET` (`openssl rand -base64 48`) in `apps/web/.env` after copying.

```sh
pnpm install
for f in apps/api apps/web packages/db; do cp "$f/.env.example" "$f/.env"; done
pnpm services:up
pnpm db:migrate
pnpm dev
```

Open http://localhost:3000 and sign in with any username through the mock SSO provider.
